Cloud Library New

Your plan sets now follow you. Open a set at the office, pick it up on the laptop or iPad — same document, same takeoffs, synced automatically in the background. Documents are matched by their content, so the same PDF is recognized as the same document on every device, even after edits.

Steadier live collaboration on big sets Improved

Multi-sheet sessions got a rebuilt engine under the hood: each sheet now syncs independently, so two estimators working a 200-page set see each other's markups faster and more reliably.

PDF compression, PDF/A, and Office conversion New

Shrink bloated scan sets down to emailable sizes, export archival PDF/A for closeout, and drop Word/Excel/PowerPoint files straight into the app — they convert to PDF and join your set.

Markup rotation + editing polish Improved

Rotate any markup freely. Right-click menus, layering (bring-to-front / send-to-back), and the properties editor all got cleaned up, and the sidebar is now resizable.

Support center & seat management New

A proper support portal is live — submit questions or bug reports from the website and a human replies, usually same day. Companies on team plans can now have seats assigned and managed per person.

Set as default PDF app on macOS New

Double-click any PDF in Finder and it opens in Groundwork Takeoff.

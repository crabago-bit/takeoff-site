The tour

Everything between
plans and a winning bid.

Measure everything.
Miss nothing.

Six measurement modes — Linear, Perimeter, Area, Volume, Count, and Dimension — with snap, smart scale detection, and dimension chains. Set the scale once; every takeoff on the sheet stays honest.

  • Formula evaluator — type 12*8+4 anywhere a quantity goes
  • Smart scale & dimension chains keep multi-sheet sets consistent
  • Snap modes and crosshair for precise vertex placement

Quantities become dollars. Instantly.

Takeoff and estimating live in the same window. Arm an assembly from your library, draw, and watch materials, labor, and totals build themselves in the live estimate grid. Then send it to Excel, CSV, or your estimating system — one click, batch export included.

  • Assembly Builder — model your crews' real cost structure
  • Materials catalog with auto-apply on next draw
  • Excel, CSV, and batch export — bid-ready output

AI that counts
so you don't have to.

AI Detect scans the sheet and suggests counts and measurements — fixtures, outlets, doors, whatever repeats. You review, accept, and move on. AI-assisted redaction and measure suggestions come standard, not as a five-figure add-on.

  • AI Detect for repeated symbols and quantities
  • AI measure suggestions with human review — you stay in control
  • Built-in OCR turns scanned sheets into searchable text

Rev B changed. See exactly where.

Plan Compare overlays two revisions on the live canvas. Diff mode paints removals red and additions blue; Blend mode weighs both revisions with an opacity slider. Auto-align snaps sheets together using text anchors — or drag to align by hand.

  • Diff and Blend modes on the live canvas — not a flattened export
  • Auto-align with manual dx/dy/scale override
  • Catch scope creep before it eats your margin

A full revision toolset,
not just a pen.

Revision clouds, revision deltas, and a living Revision Log that records who changed what, when — plus stamps, signatures, highlighters, notes, redaction, a reusable Tool Chest, and color legends. When Rev C lands, cloud the changes, log them, and overlay the sheets to prove them.

  • Revision clouds & deltas — the vocabulary your GCs already speak
  • Revision Log panel — a change history with an audit trail
  • Tool Chest — save any markup as a reusable team tool
  • Pairs with plan-compare overlay to verify every delta

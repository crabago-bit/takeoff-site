When Kreo wins

We'll say it plainly.

Kreo's AI is the most autonomous in the category, full stop. Auto Measure 2.0 finds rooms, doors, windows, and wall finishes on clean vector plans; Caddie has evolved into a genuine in-app agent that plans tasks, builds BOQs, and generates reports; the new "Skills" playbooks let you script AI workflows without code. Their support is praised for sub-hour responses, and unlimited free guest seats are generous. If you're a high-volume estimator at a desk with fiber internet and clean CAD-exported PDFs, Kreo Pro is a legitimate choice.

But that AI costs $2,100 per seat per year — Groundwork includes AI Detect and measure suggestions on every seat at $950, a difference of $1,150 per seat, every year. And ours runs native on the Mac or PC in front of you, keeps working offline for 14 days, and treats AI as a suggestion for a human to verify — so there's no cleanup phase after the magic.
